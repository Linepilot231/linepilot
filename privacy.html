Privacy Policy for Line Pilot

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Line Pilot helps users view and report estimated wait times and crowd conditions for restaurants, coffee shops, banks, bars, nightclubs and other locations.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Line Pilot, we may collect:

  • Location information: Your device location may be used to show nearby businesses, estimate distances and provide relevant wait time information.
  • Contact information: We may collect information such as your email address when you create an account, contact support or submit information through the app.
  • User-submitted information: We may collect wait-time reports, crowd reports, favorites and other information you voluntarily submit.
  • Technical information: We may collect basic device, diagnostic and usage information needed to maintain, secure and improve the app.

How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide nearby location and wait-time features.
  • Display and improve crowd and wait-time estimates.
  • Send notifications requested by the user.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve Line Pilot.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud and security problems.
  • Respond to questions and support requests.

Location Permissions

Line Pilot only accesses location information when permission is granted through your device settings. You may disable location access at any time through the Settings application on your device. Some app features may not work correctly when location access is disabled.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the app, such as hosting, mapping, analytics, notification and infrastructure providers. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect users, the app or the public.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the security of Line Pilot.

Your Choices

You may manage location and notification permissions through your device settings. You may also contact us to ask questions about your information or request deletion of information associated with you.

Children's Privacy

Line Pilot is not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
